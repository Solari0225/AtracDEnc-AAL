The project's own build compiles ATRAC3 and ATRAC3plus subsets; no libsndfile, FFmpeg,
Media Foundation, installed Sony codec or .NET is used at runtime.

filter/Huffman numerical tables retain the upstream FFmpeg LGPL attribution.

expose the final emitted spectrum for an additional independent parser check.

2048-sample AAL enhancement support are in core/plus and compression.

Prebuilt binary support runtimes
The binaries were built with Zig 0.14.1. Statically linked C++ standard-library,
ABI and unwind support come from LLVM libc++, libc++abi and libunwind under
Apache-2.0 with LLVM exceptions. Windows CRT support includes MinGW-w64 code;
its component notices are reproduced in licenses/MinGW-w64-COPYING.txt.
Zig runtime support is under the MIT (Expat) license. Full upstream license
texts are reproduced in licenses/. The compiler is a build-time tool only.

Sony, Walkman and ATRAC names identify interoperability targets only.
Sony software was an external research oracle. Its executables, libraries,
instruction captures and private analysis tools are absent from this package.
The conformance fixtures are encoded data generated from synthetic audio,
not executable code. They run without Sony software.
